Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. For emergency water extraction in Marietta, Marietta Restoration brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

Why Water Extraction Is Not a DIY Job

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. We go after those hidden deposits with specialty tools and methods, and that takes out far more water than extracting the surface only. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
  • Carpeting so wet it squelches when you step on it or has puddles on top
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Store a bucket and spare towels where you can grab them, along with the number for a restoration company
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
